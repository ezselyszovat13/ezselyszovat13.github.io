01 About

A few things about me.

From a small Hungarian village to enterprise Java teams — here's how I got here.

5+
Years of
experience

The biggest achievement goes to my parents, who made it possible to get a first name which is part of my hometown. Therefore I just became Szovát from Bágyogszovát.

The connection between me and my profession started in secondary school where I learned C# as my main language. I also participated in some competitions to build confidence for the years to come. After my fifth year, I acquired my trade — though I did not finish my studies there.

I was admitted to ELTE, where I studied Computer Science. I picked up comprehensive knowledge in various subjects, but considered C++ and PHP (Laravel) my most valuable picks. For one semester I was giving weekly lectures and supervising exam tasks for Object-Oriented Programming in C++.

In the last year of my BSc, I joined Leviathan Solutions, where I was part of a team developing a nationwide access-control system. I experienced many types of problems there — debugging, writing automated tests, manual tests, and developing new features.

After my BSc I started an MSc in Data Science, but preferred the work experience I was gathering. At the end of November I was lucky to join EPAM Systems as a Software Engineer, where I learned a lot of exciting concepts, developed in Clean Code and Clean Design, and got the chance to mentor colleagues.

Then I moved to Morgan Stanley, where I dug deeper into Java, filled gaps in my knowledge, and brought programming concepts and Scrum methodologies into our projects. Alongside the professional growth, I got my driving license, got married, and welcomed our child within those 2.5 years.

These altered circumstances led to a move to the countryside and to looking for full-remote opportunities — which Adroit Group could provide with up-to-date and relevant technologies. At Adroit I have already shipped several meaningful projects that can be observed below. On top of the hands-on delivery, I have specialised in development with Claude Code and hold a CCAF certification, which keeps me at the cutting edge of AI-assisted software development.

When I'm not in front of a computer, I like skipping rope or playing basketball. I'm also slightly addicted to escape rooms — with a pleasant escaping percentage. :)
